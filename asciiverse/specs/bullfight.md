# Bullfight: rules spec

Written 2026-11-14 from the idea of the book's game, without its listing or black-box play.

## Our design

A bloodless bullfight, after the Portuguese corrida and the bloodless
fights of California's Portuguese communities: no swords, nothing that
harms the bull, who leaves the ring alive, led out by steers. Nothing is
shown of injuries; a matador who is caught is tossed onto the sand and
helped up or out by the team.

- Each afternoon brings a new bull with a name, a weight (460 to 580 kg),
  bravery 1 to 5 (how true he charges; the crowd loves a brave bull) and
  speed 1 to 5 (which makes him more dangerous), and a team of capemen of
  one of four qualities.
- Three stages. With the big cape, up to four passes; with the small red
  muleta, up to six; then the finale. The player may end the cape or
  muleta stage early.
- The passes, each with a danger and an applause value: with the cape,
  the Veronica (slow and close, 0.13 and 10), the Larga (wide, 0.045 and
  4) and the Media veronica (a closing flourish, 0.08 and 7); with the
  muleta, the Natural (left hand, 0.16 and 13), the Derechazo (right
  hand, 0.10 and 8.5) and the Pase de pecho (a closing flourish, 0.085
  and 7.5).
- The chance of being caught is the pass's danger times the bull's speed
  (0.7 + 0.12 a point), his freshness (0.5 + 0.7 of his strength left),
  the run of daring passes since the last flourish (35 percent more for
  each) and the team's help (1.15 - 0.1 a level). Up to 2.2 times that
  chance, the horns pass close: the crowd gasps and the pass earns 30
  percent more.
- Applause for a clean pass is its value times the bull's bravery (0.6 +
  0.15 a point), the run of passes (20 percent a pass, up to four), 1.2
  with the muleta, and chance (0.8 to 1.2); the same pass three times in
  a row earns only 60 percent.
- Every charge costs the bull 7 to 12 of his 100 strength. Below 12 he
  stops charging and the finale comes.
- Caught once, the matador gets up to a roar (5 points for courage) and
  carries on. Caught twice, the team helps the matador out to rest, the
  score so far counts 60 percent plus 6, and the steers lead the bull
  out.
- The finale: the forcados' catch, where eight men meet the bull's
  charge and hold him, or the steers. The catch succeeds with chance
  0.3 + 0.08 a team level + 0.35 of the strength the bull has spent, 12
  percent more for each try after the first, and earns 18 plus 2 a point
  of bravery; a miss earns 2 for the try. Three tries at most; then the
  steers.
- The ovation is the score: under 25 polite silence, to 49 warm
  applause, to 79 a lap of the ring, to 109 flowers and hats, from 110
  carried out on shoulders. A bull of bravery 4 or 5 is applauded on his
  way out.
- Kept in the state folder: afternoons fought, the best ovation, times
  carried out.
